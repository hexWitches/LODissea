The Project

LODissea maps the cultural connections across Europe, exploring the vast archives of the Europeana database. We navigate through digital artifacts, surfacing hidden narratives and shared histories.

The Team

A multidisciplinary group of researchers, designers, and developers passionate about digital cultural heritage and semantic web technologies.
